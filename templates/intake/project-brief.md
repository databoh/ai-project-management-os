# Project Brief Template

## Purpose

Capture enough context to route and begin responsible discovery while preserving the difference between the original request, verified information, interpretation, and proposed solution.

## Usage

Copy this file for one initiative, replace guidance values, and retain `Not provided`, `Not established`, or `Not applicable` where information is genuinely unavailable. Do not manufacture completeness. Link to controlled source material rather than duplicating sensitive content.

## Completion checks

- Raw request and interpretation are separate.
- Facts have sources; assumptions use stable IDs.
- Requester and decision owner are not assumed to be the same.
- Dates and budgets include source, flexibility, and approval status.
- Discovery objective is tied to a decision.
- G0 result and gaps are recorded.
