# Discovery Report Template

## Purpose

Synthesize discovery evidence into a decision-ready recommendation and a transparent G1 quality-gate result.

## Usage

Write for the accountable decision owner. Summarize evidence instead of pasting research notes. Link to controlled sources, preserve contradictory findings, and use `Not established` where discovery did not produce sufficient evidence.

## Completion checks

- The report distinguishes findings, interpretation, recommendation, and approval.
- Material findings include sources, dates, confidence, and limitations.
- Disconfirming evidence and viable alternatives are preserved.
- Critical assumptions and residual risks have owners.
- The recommendation is tied to the decision and desired outcome.
- G1 exceptions have an authorized approver and expiry.
- Scope, budget, and dates are not presented as committed Phase 2 outputs.
