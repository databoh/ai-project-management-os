# Product Requirements Document Template

## Purpose

Align product, design, engineering, data, operations, and stakeholders on the problem, users, outcomes, scope, experience, product behavior, measurement, and decision boundaries.

## Usage

Use only when a shared product specification adds value beyond the product-definition modules and requirement register. Reference authoritative IDs and links; avoid copying full BRD, FRD, SRS, journey, or persona content.

## Completion checks

- Problem, users, outcomes, scope, and measures are traceable.
- Product behavior and experience direction remain distinct from implementation design.
- MVP includes measurement, operations, recovery, and mandatory controls.
- Requirements reference authoritative IDs and acceptance evidence.
- Rollout direction is not presented as a delivery commitment.
- Approval status and remaining G3 inputs are explicit.
