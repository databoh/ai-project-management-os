# Business Requirements Document Template

## Purpose

Establish the business need, desired outcomes, capabilities, rules, scope direction, stakeholders, constraints, and approval basis for an initiative. Use a BRD when business-level alignment or obligation requires a controlled artifact.

## Usage

Reference the authoritative requirement register by ID. Do not copy detailed product, functional, or software requirements into this document. Replace defaults with evidence-backed content and retain `Not established` for unresolved decisions.

## Completion checks

- The business need, outcome, and decision requested are explicit.
- Business requirements are necessary, sourced, and outcome-linked.
- Product or system detail is referenced rather than duplicated.
- Benefits identify method, owner, uncertainty, and measurement horizon.
- Constraints, obligations, assumptions, and risks remain distinguishable.
- Approval does not imply an unreviewed scope, cost, or schedule commitment.
